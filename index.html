Contact Padel Club

For app help, account questions, or a privacy request, email quintin@seagullcompany.com. Include your app version and a description of the problem. Please do not email passwords or verification codes.

How do I start a tournament?

Add your players in the player library, then select New tournament. Choose 4, 8 or 12 players and a fixed match total. Courts are assigned automatically. Every player partners with every other player once.

How are points and ratings different?

Each partner receives their team's match points. The player with the most tournament points leads the standings; wins break a points tie. Doubles Elo estimates playing strength across completed tournaments and helps balance future matches.

Can I correct a score?

Tap a match score to edit it. If the event is finished, choose Reopen to correct scores, make your changes, then finish again. Ratings are recalculated from the completed history.

Where are my games saved?

Without an account, your workspace is saved on your device. Signing in opens a separate private cloud workspace. To transfer device-only games, export a backup first, sign in, then import it into an empty cloud workspace.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings, select Delete organizer account. Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm. This removes your account and its active cloud workspace. Export anything you want to keep first. You can also contact support for assistance.

Your information

Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Padel Club version 1.0

This policy explains how Padel Club handles information when you use its tournament app and this support website. For privacy questions, contact quintin@seagullcompany.com.

Information you provide

  • Organizer account email and authentication information, processed by Supabase Auth.
  • Player names or nicknames and any organizer notes you choose to enter.
  • Tournament names, rosters, schedules, scores and the ratings derived from results.
  • Information you send when contacting support.

Players do not need individual accounts. Organizers should have permission to record other players' information and should only include information needed for their games.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, save your player library and games, calculate standings and ratings, synchronize your workspace and respond to support requests. The app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking features.

Storage and service providers

Device-only workspaces remain on your device. When signed in, the app stores your account's workspace with Supabase. Cloud access is restricted to the relevant organizer account through database access controls; authorized service operators and providers may access information as needed to operate and support the service. Connections to Supabase use HTTPS.

The current cloud database is hosted in the United States. Supabase handles authentication and cloud storage. Google Gmail delivers account confirmation and other authentication emails, which include your email address and the message content. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and operational logs, to deliver and protect their services. Their policies are available at Supabase Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and retention

You can edit player details and correct scores in the app, export a workspace, or delete your organizer account in Settings. Account deletion removes the active account and its cloud workspace, as well as that account's workspace and recovery snapshots on the device making the request. Copies exported or saved on other devices are separate and must be removed there. Provider backups and operational logs may remain for their normal retention periods.

Device-only records remain until the app's local data is removed, subject to any device backups. Signing out does not erase saved local workspaces. Contact support if you need help accessing, correcting or deleting information.

Updates

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Padel Club

For app help, membership questions, or a privacy request, email quintin@seagullcompany.com. Include your app version and a description of the problem. Please do not email passwords or verification codes.

How do I start a tournament?

Add your players in the player library, then select New tournament. Choose 4, 8 or 12 players and a fixed match total. Courts are assigned automatically. Every player partners with every other player once.

How are points and ratings different?

Each partner receives their team's match points. The player with the most tournament points leads the standings; wins break a points tie. Doubles Elo estimates playing strength across completed tournaments and helps balance future matches.

Can I correct a score?

Tap a match score to edit it. If the event is finished, choose Reopen to correct scores, make your changes, then finish again. Ratings are recalculated from the completed history.

Where are my games saved?

As a guest, your games are saved on your device. Members of a facility club share its player roster and games with equal permissions. Choose Become a member, find your player name, and register or sign in to link your existing history. Device games remain separate.

How do I leave or delete my membership?

In Settings, you can leave your club or delete your membership and sign-in. Deleting your sign-in requires your password and DELETE. Shared player profiles and game history remain for the club. Contact support for corrections, mistaken profile claims or privacy requests.

Your information

Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Padel Club version 1.0

This policy explains how Padel Club handles information when you use its tournament app and this support website. For privacy questions, contact quintin@seagullcompany.com.

Information you provide

  • Personal membership email and authentication information, processed by Supabase Auth.
  • Player names or nicknames and any shared club notes you choose to enter.
  • Tournament names, rosters, schedules, scores and the ratings derived from results.
  • Information you send when contacting support.

Players can appear on a club roster before they join the app. Members should have permission to record other players’ information and should only include details needed for their games. Player names are available in the joining search; notes, emails and full club records are not exposed to guests. Profile claims are self-attested, and email confirmation does not verify a player’s real-world identity.

How information is used

Information is used to provide membership access, save your player library and games, calculate standings and ratings, synchronize your club and respond to support requests. The app does not include advertising or cross-app tracking features.

Storage and service providers

Device-only workspaces remain on your device. When linked to a club, members share its play records in Supabase. Database access controls give linked club members equal read/write access; personal sign-in credentials and membership links remain protected. Authorized service operators and providers may access information as needed to operate and support the service. Connections to Supabase use HTTPS.

The current cloud database is hosted in the United States. Supabase handles authentication and cloud storage. Google Gmail delivers membership confirmation and other authentication emails, which include your email address and the message content. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and operational logs, to deliver and protect their services. Their policies are available at Supabase Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and retention

You can edit player details and correct scores in the app, export a club backup, leave a club, or delete your membership and sign-in in Settings. Deletion removes personal authentication and membership links, plus the requesting person’s local club caches and recovery snapshots on that device. Shared players, games and history remain for other members. Private migration recovery copies and operation fingerprints may be retained for data recovery and integrity. Copies exported or saved on other devices are separate and must be removed there. Provider backups and operational logs may remain for their normal retention periods.

Device-only records remain until the app's local data is removed, subject to any device backups. Signing out does not erase saved local workspaces. Contact support if you need help accessing, correcting or deleting information.

Updates

Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
